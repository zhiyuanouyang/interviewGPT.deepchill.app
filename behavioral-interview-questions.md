## The STAR Framework (and Why It's Not Enough)

Most guides stop at STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result). But at senior level, interviewers are evaluating something deeper:

- **Situation**: Was it genuinely high-stakes? Did you volunteer for it or were you assigned?
- **Task**: What was your specific ownership? Did you show senior-level judgment?
- **Action**: Did you influence, lead, or just execute? What trade-offs did you make?
- **Result**: Quantified impact. And — crucially — what did you **learn**?

Top candidates go beyond STAR with **STARL** — adding a **Learnings** component that signals self-awareness and growth mindset.

## How to Prepare

**Step 1: Build a story bank** — 8–10 strong stories from your career, each covering: a challenge, your specific role, a decision you made, and a measurable result.

**Step 2: Map stories to themes** — Each story should cover at least 2–3 themes (e.g., a conflict story can also demonstrate technical leadership and prioritization).

**Step 3: Practice out loud** — Behavioral answers should be delivered in 2–3 minutes. Anything longer loses the interviewer.

**Step 4: Prepare your "failure" story first** — It's the hardest one to tell well, and it's almost always asked.
